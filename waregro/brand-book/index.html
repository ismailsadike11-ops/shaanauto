01 · Our brand

Buy your stock.
Grow your business.

WAREGRO is the e-commerce marketplace where shop owners, retailers and resellers buy their stock online, from trusted suppliers, at fair prices and delivered to their door.

The name says it all: WARE — the goods, the stock, the box — and GRO — growth. Every order on WAREGRO is a step forward for the business behind it.

من نحن

ويرغرو منصة تجارة إلكترونية تمكّن أصحاب المحلات والتجار من شراء سلعهم أونلاين من موردين موثوقين، بأسعار عادلة وتوصيل حتى باب المحل.

الاسم يختصر الفكرة: WARE تعني السلعة والصندوق، وGRO تعني النمو. كل طلبية على ويرغرو خطوة إلى الأمام لتجارتك.

05 · Logo mark

Built on the box

The mark is drawn on an isometric grid with a single stroke weight and rounded caps and joins. The stroke is 7.4% of the mark's width, so the mark stays bold even at small sizes.

The W and the arrow are always in Mystic Red. The box is in Deep Ember, or in the colour of the background version (see page 10).

07

05 · Word mark

One name, two scripts

The Latin word mark is set in heavy geometric capitals with open tracking, the same family of letters as the Waresouq system.

الكلمة العربية «ويرغرو» مكتوبة بخط هندسي، مع تمديد الكشيدة ليتساوى عرضها مع الكلمة اللاتينية.

Both word marks are supplied as outlined vector files. Never re-type the logo in a font. The Arabic and Latin word marks are always the same width when used together.

08 · Clear space & minimum size

Give it room

Keep a clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter W in the word mark (marked x). No text, image or edge may enter this area.

Thin · Light · Regular · Medium · Bold

Light · Regular · Medium · SemiBold · Bold

Available free from Google Fonts. The font files are in the fonts folder of this kit.

Pattern & arrow

The mark repeats as a brand pattern for packaging, backgrounds and social posts. Use it at 6–12% opacity so it never competes with content.

The rising arrow can be used alone as a graphic accent: next to numbers, growth figures and calls to action.
